Resume Screening

Bulk-screen a local folder of PDF/DOCX resumes by experience and required keywords. Originals are never modified.

1. Folder and criteria

Same-machine local path, e.g. /data/resumes. Only top-level .pdf / .docx files are scanned; subfolders and other extensions are ignored. No OCR in v1.

Screening criteria

Each criterion is optional, but at least one is required to start.

    Extensible: future criteria (education, location, titles, …) plug in here without workflow redesign.

    Select a valid folder with resumes and set at least one criterion to start.
